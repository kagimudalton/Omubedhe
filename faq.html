Frequently Asked Questions

General
Mubedhe Driving University is a Ministry-licensed driving school in Uganda, certified by the Ministry of Works & Transport and the Ministry of Internal Affairs. We offer professional driver training and licence processing for all vehicle classes.
Yes. We are fully licensed by two government ministries: the Ministry of Works & Transport and the Ministry of Internal Affairs. Our licence covers training and processing permits for all classes of drivers.
We have 8 branches: Kyanja (Head Office), Mukono Wantoni, Kalagi, Makindye, Nansana, Kayunga, Buloba, and Entebbe. See our Contact page for full addresses.
Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Saturday: 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays, though weekend and night training programmes are available by arrangement.
Enrolment & Documents
Ugandans need their National ID (NIN). Refugees need a Refugee ID. Foreigners need a valid Passport. Parent or guardian IDs are also required for all applicants.
Fill in the registration form on our website, or WhatsApp us on +256 744 253 209. We will call you within 24 hours to confirm your branch, date and package. You then pay and bring your ID and receipt to the office.
Yes — you can submit your details through the form on our homepage. We confirm everything by phone within 24 hours. Full payment is made in person at the branch.
The minimum age is 18 years for car and heavy vehicle training, and 16 years for motorcycle training (Class A1/B1), subject to parental consent.
Training Packages
Our most affordable package is the Express Refresher at UGX 280,000 for 7 days — ideal for returning drivers. For complete beginners, the Team 5:1 at UGX 400,000 for 14 days is the best value.
It depends on the package. Express Refresher: 7 days. Solo Refresher: max 5 days. Team 5:1: 14 days. Solo 30: 30 days. Solo 2-Hour Premium: 20 days. Engineering training varies.
This package gives you 2 hours of one-on-one training every day for 20 days. Your instructor picks you up from your home location. It is our most intensive and comprehensive package at UGX 1,000,000.
Yes. We have a Weekend & Night Programme for UGX 400,000 over 8 days, designed for people who work during the week. We also offer home location training at UGX 600,000 for 14 days.
Yes. We offer Manual Express Upgrade (5 days, UGX 350,000), Heavy Team 5:1 (14 days, UGX 450,000), and other heavy vehicle packages. See the Packages section on our homepage.
Licences & Permits
The normal process takes about 36 days to book the IOV (Inspector of Vehicles) test. Your Certificate of Competence is issued 5 days after the IOV test. All balances must be cleared before completion.
The IOV test fee is UGX 25,000. This is separate from your training package fee. We guide you through the entire process.
New application: UGX 350,000. Learner's licence: UGX 60,000. 1-year permit: UGX 135,000. 3-year permit: UGX 300,000. 5-year permit: UGX 330,000. See our Licence Fees section for full details.
Yes. International permits are available: 1-year at UGX 135,000, 3-year at UGX 300,000, and 5-year at UGX 330,000.
We offer a Solo Refresher package (max 5 days, UGX 350,000) specifically designed for drivers with lapsed or expired permits.
Payment
All payments are made via bank transfer or mobile money. Contact us on +256 744 253 209 or WhatsApp to receive payment details. Bring your proof of payment to the office to receive your official receipt.
Yes. A receipt is issued at the office for every payment made. Always ensure you receive your receipt.
No hidden fees. There is a Contingency Fee of UGX 135,000 which covers all administrative and background processing costs for your file. This is clearly stated upfront. Hostel accommodation is available at UGX 200,000 for out-of-town students.
